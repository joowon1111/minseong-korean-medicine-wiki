BodyParts3D는 성인 남성 참고 해부 모형이며 개인별 해부학을 재현하지 않습니다.

Geometry adaptation: human-atlas (ashemag/human-atlas) based geometry, as identified in the user-provided HTML.
https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas/blob/main/public/ATTRIBUTION.md
The supplied HTML packs simplified, quantized geometry into layer buckets. This integration separates the buckets into model.json, retains the supplied geometry and approximate surface coordinates, and connects point descriptions to the archive. It does not certify coordinates or add needle paths.

Point descriptions and source links: existing Minseong archive acupoint discovery data. Follow each point's source links for provenance. Approximate display coordinates come from the supplied HTML, not from a verified 3D coordinate standard.
